Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about counseling? Find answers below.

Are counseling sessions truly confidential?
Yes, absolutely. Everything discussed during your session remains private between you and your counselor, adhering strictly to professional ethics.
How much do counseling sessions cost?
All sessions provided by the Thika TTI Peer Counseling Club and resident counselors are completely free for registered students.
Can I choose between physical and online meetings?
Yes! When booking your appointment, you can choose either an in-person campus office visit or an online Google Meet session.
